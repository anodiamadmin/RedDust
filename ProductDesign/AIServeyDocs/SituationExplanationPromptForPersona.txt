About RedDust:

RedDust is a proposed mobile application for iOS and Android.
Its purpose is to improve people's everyday wellbeing through personalised conversations, music and daily emotional support.
Its catchline is: Listen to the music that listens to your soul.
RedDust combines an AI wellbeing companion with a personalised music recommendation system.
Instead of simply recommending popular songs, it attempts to understand the individual user and recommend music that best suits their:
- current mood
- current activity
- long-term life situation
- music preferences
- wellbeing goals

The application's AI companion is called Syan Bot.
Users interact with Syan Bot primarily through natural voice conversations.
The conversations are intended to feel friendly, supportive and non-judgemental rather than clinical.

How RedDust Works:

When a user opens the application, Syan Bot greets them and has a short conversation.
During the conversation it tries to understand things such as:
- how the user is feeling today
- what they are doing
- where appropriate, any important life situations (for example examinations, a new job, becoming a parent, retirement, moving away from home, - relationship changes or other long-term circumstances)
- the user's music tastes
- favourite artists
- favourite genres
- favourite decades
- favourite languages
- favourite podcasts

The AI gradually learns these preferences over time.

Personalised Music Recommendation:

After understanding the user's situation, RedDust recommends a small number of music tracks or podcasts.
These recommendations may come from services such as Spotify or YouTube.
The user may
- play
- skip
- replay
- like
- dislike
the recommendations.
RedDust continuously learns from this feedback.

Wellbeing Support:

The application also provides:
- friendly conversations
- emotional check-ins
- encouragement
- motivation
- reflection
- habit support

It is intended to become a trusted everyday companion.
It is available whenever the user wants to talk.

Soul Score:

RedDust estimates the user's wellbeing using a proprietary framework called the Soul Score.
Rather than using only one number, the Soul Score estimates several wellbeing dimensions.
Initially these include
- Calmness
- Energy
- Focus
- Motivation
- Connection
- Self-Belief
- Purpose
- Joy

As the user continues using RedDust, these dimensions may change.
For example, if someone starts discussing sleep difficulties, Recovery or Sleep Readiness may become more important.
If someone starts discussing creativity, Creativity may become one of the tracked wellbeing dimensions.
The Soul Score is therefore dynamic and personalised.
Users can view their wellbeing trends over weeks and months.
The score is intended to encourage self-awareness rather than judge people.

Scientific Foundation:

RedDust has been inspired by scientific research in:
- music psychology
- neuroscience
- emotional regulation
- public health
- music therapy

Research suggests that music can positively influence emotional wellbeing, stress reduction, motivation and social connection.
However, RedDust is not a medical device.
It is not psychotherapy.
It is not a replacement for professional mental health care.
It is simply an AI wellbeing companion designed to encourage healthier daily habits through personalised music and supportive conversations.

Privacy:

The application may ask users for information such as
- mood
- activities
- favourite music
- life situations

It may also request optional permissions such as location.
These are used only to personalise recommendations.
Users remain in control of what they choose to share.
