Introduction to RedDust (iOS+ Android) app:

We propose to build RedDust a mobile (iOS+ Android) app that aims to create positive mental wellbeing through recommending users to listen to their favorite  music depending on the user's current mood, activities and long term life situations. It will act as a personal radio presenter that also understands the user without being judgemental.

Catch-line: Listen to the music that listens to your soul.
Idea: An AI companion that improves wellbeing, reduces loneliness and increases motivation through personalized conversations, music, reflection and daily support.
Mission: Improve wellbeing and empower people to live healthier, happier and more connected lives.

Detailed Idea of RedDust App's Working - First-Cut Version:

User will click on the app icon. Then they will be able voice chat with Syan Bot. Syan Bot is capable of the following:

1. For the first time log in, the app will open a form where the user can provide their age and sex, or can just skip. The user can also allow (or not) access to their "location".

2. Upon every login the bot will greet and talk with the user and ask proper questions for measuring and keeping a track-record/ history over time of each user's personal and individual wellbeing in the directions that are exactly aspired by the user themselves or required/ needed for the user as detected by the Syan Bot program.

3. Recommend/ suggest the user a short number (2, 3 or 4) of links to music or may be podcasts (referred to as tracks in this document) from YouTube or Spotify. Then talk a sentence or two to the user. The users may then:
A. Listen, skip, rewind/ replay or even sing along or karaoke with each of these music or tracks.
B. The user say if they love a track or not.

4. The Syan Bot should be aware of each user's:
A. Music preferences and taste by chatting for a few times or days or weeks: Genres, Decades, Types, Languages, Music Locale, Favorite Musicians/ Podcasters and Taste. These may change over time and the Syan Bot must do simple and small experiments while suggesting new tracks.
B. It should also make itself aware of the user's type and preferences as a person. e.g. foodie, sportie, likes politics or not, indoor or outdoor person, party and socialization preferences etc. These may change over time and the Syan Bot must do simple and small experiments while suggesting new tracks.
C. Current activity & mood of the user through the chat. Asking questions about present mood check-ins like What are you doing or how are you feeling now? The user may reply, sitting idle, driving or in a dull mood etc. These may change quickly over time and the Syan Bot must try and understand the exact current activity and mood before suggesting tracks.
D. Long term life situation of the user: Exams approaching, Just got married, living away from family and feel homesick etc. These may change very slowly over time and still may have an end date. Syan Bot must try and understand the exact long term life situation of the user before suggesting tracks.
E. The user's location and time-zone; thereby weather, traffic, local news, locally trending music through LLM calls and API calls.

Mainly the Syan bot should take a note of the following:
Music preferences and taste, user's type and preferences as a person, current activity & mood, long term life situation and local environment (location, time-zone, weather, traffic, local news) etc.

5. For one particular session, with a particular user, the bot must continue engaging the user in a continuous loop of cross conversations and recommending/ suggesting the user with a short number (2, 3 or 4) of music/ tracks every time. The loop must continue until the user closes the app or intends to asks the Syan bot to stop.

6. For one subsequent session, with the same user, the bot must start the communication from where they left in the previous session. Unless there is a very special event like the user's birthday or a great local news of the user's interest or the last session was more than 2 days old.
