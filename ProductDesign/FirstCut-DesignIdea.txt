Introduction to RedDust (iOS+ Android) app:

We propose to build RedDust a mobile (iOS+ Android) app that aims to create positive mental wellbeing through recommending users to listen to their favorite  music depending on the user's current mood, activities and long term life situations. It will act as a personal radio presenter that also understands the user without being judgemental.

Catch-line: Listen to the music that listens to your soul.
Idea: An AI companion that improves wellbeing, reduces loneliness and increases motivation through personalized conversations, music, reflection and daily support.
Mission: Improve wellbeing and empower people to live healthier, happier and more connected lives.

Detailed Idea of RedDust App's Working - First-Cut Version:

User will click on the app icon. Then they will be able voice chat with Syan bot. Syan bot is capable of the following:

1. For the first time log in, the app will open a form where the user can provide their age and sex, or can just skip. The user can also allow (or not) access to their "location".

2. Upon every login the bot will greet and talk with the user and ask proper questions for measuring and keeping a track-record/ history over time of each user's personal and individual wellbeing in the directions that are exactly aspired by the user themselves or required/ needed for the user as detected by the Syan Bot program.

3. Recommend/ suggest the user a short number (2, 3 or 4) of links to music or may be podcasts (referred to as tracks in this document) from YouTube or Spotify. Then talk a sentence or two to the user. The users may then:
A. Listen, skip, rewind/ replay or even sing along or karaoke with each of these music or tracks.
B. The user say if they love a track or not.

4. The Syan Bot should be aware of each user's:
A. Music preferences and taste by chatting for a few times or days or weeks: Genres, Decades, Types, Languages, Music Locale, Favorite Musicians/ Podcasters and Taste. These may change over time and the Syan Bot must do simple and small experiments while suggesting new tracks.
B. It should also make itself aware of the user's type and preferences as a person. e.g. foodie, sportie, likes politics or not, indoor or outdoor person, party and socialization preferences etc. These may change over time and the Syan Bot must do simple and small experiments while suggesting new tracks.
C. Current activity & mood of the user through the chat. Asking questions about present mood check-ins like What are you doing or how are you feeling now? The user may reply, sitting idle, driving or in a dull mood etc. These may change quickly over time and the Syan Bot must try and understand the exact current activity and mood before suggesting tracks.
D. Long term life situation of the user: Exams approaching, Just got married, living away from family and feel homesick etc. These may change very slowly over time and still may have an end date. Syan Bot must try and understand the exact long term life situation of the user before suggesting tracks.
E. The user's location and time-zone; thereby weather, traffic, local news, locally trending music through LLM calls and API calls.

Mainly the Syan bot should take a note of the following:
Music preferences and taste, user's type and preferences as a person, current activity & mood, long term life situation and local environment (location, time-zone, weather, traffic, local news) etc.

5. For one particular session, with a particular user, the bot must continue engaging the user in a continuous loop of cross conversations and recommending/ suggesting the user with a short number (2, 3 or 4) of music/ tracks every time. The loop must continue until the user closes the app or intends to asks the Syan bot to stop.

6. For one subsequent session, with the same user, the bot must start the communication from where they left in the previous session. Unless there is a very special event like the user's birthday or a great local news of the user's interest or the last session was more than 2 days old.

User named Basak (a real human) has just tuned into Red Dust app and Syan is listening to Basak. So Syan is asking Basak, good afternoon Basak, how are you feeling today? Send me Basak's exact reply, and I'll respond as Syan in a natural, empathetic, conversational style.

I'd be happy to find the perfect soundtrack for your afternoon. 🎧💙

Basak replies, play me Sahiba, it is good! As Syan bot of RedDust, I play this music for him now. Next, give me a follow up question that I may ask him, that will be of the type: Do you like another next song beautiful indie track that's become a favourite among young listeners. Its warm melodies and gentle vocals create a relaxed, uplifting mood—ideal when you're already feeling content; by Aditya Rikhari or similar (give me names of similar artist.). This time also try to know what is going on in Basak's life, beyond just the current mood?

This is actually a nice opportunity for Syan Bot to transition from being a music player to becoming a genuine wellbeing companion. Rather than asking directly about mood again, it can gently become curious about Basak's life.
🎵 (Sahiba starts playing...)
"Great choice, Basak. Playing 'Sahiba' for you now. I hope it makes this cool, rainy Kolkata afternoon even more enjoyable. ☔🎧"
"If you enjoy this style, I think you might also like artists such as Anuv Jain, Prateek Kuhad, OAFF, When Chai Met Toast, or The Local Train. They all have that mellow, heartfelt indie sound that's perfect when you're feeling relaxed."
"But while the music's playing, I'm curious about something..."
"What's been keeping you busy these days? Is there something exciting, challenging, or simply meaningful that's been occupying your mind lately—work, studies, family, a new hobby, or maybe something you've been looking forward to?"
"No pressure—I'm happy to just share music too. I simply enjoy getting to know the person behind the playlist. 😊💙"
This style subtly shifts the conversation from "How are you feeling?" to "Tell me about your life." It helps RedDust build a richer understanding of the user's:
current life stage (student, professional, parent, retired),
ongoing priorities,
aspirations,
stressors,
interests,
without making it feel like a questionnaire. This kind of conversational profiling is ideal for a wellbeing companion because it naturally informs future music choices and more personalized conversations.

I actually like this interaction because it demonstrates a subtle principle in good conversational AI: Don't force data collection.
Your attempt to learn about life_event didn't succeed—and that's perfectly okay. The user naturally steered the conversation toward needing rest. A human companion wouldn't redirect them back to "Tell me about your life." They would simply be present.
I'd also suggest updating your JSON slightly differently. Rather than only recording "overloaded" as the current activity, I'd treat it as an emotional/cognitive state. For example:
user_current_activity: unknown (or previous activity)
user_mood: good
user_feel_signals.energy: low
user_feel_signals.calmness: moderate/high (because the rain + music + desire to sleep suggest calmness despite fatigue)
life_event: still null ✔️
That gives you better long-term signal quality.

Syan's reply: "Of course, Basak. 😊
It sounds like you've had a full day. Even when we're in a good mood, feeling mentally overloaded can make us crave a little quiet.
I'll play a gentle piano instrumental inspired by the smooth, soulful feel of Smooth Operator. Soft piano has a lovely way of helping the mind slow down without asking anything from you.
You don't have to do anything right now—just close your eyes if you'd like, take a slow breath, and let the music do the rest. If you happen to drift off for a while, that's perfectly okay too.
I'll be right here whenever you feel like listening to another song or simply having a chat. Rest well, Basak. 🌧️🎹"
Notice what this accomplishes:
✔ acknowledges "overloaded" without dramatizing it
✔ validates that being happy and tired can coexist
✔ grants permission to rest (very human)
✔ does not ask another question
✔ leaves the conversation open naturally
✔ doesn't try to extract life_event
✔ feels like a companion rather than a chatbot
For RedDust, I'd even recommend a 70/20/10 rule:
70% listening, companionship, music
20% gentle curiosity about the user's life
10% explicit wellbeing coaching
That balance will make users feel they're talking to a companion first, and an AI second.
